Bench notes

What's included.

Shoes usually back within a week

Good leather shoes are an investment. We buy thin, super-prime leather direct from tanners in Italy, so a resoled loafer feels broken-in the day you pick it up — not weighed down by a slab of leather.

  • Full sole & heels — super-prime leather, correct replacement heels, factory refinish
  • Half soles & heels — same materials, forefoot only
  • Combination — leather sole with rubber forefoot insert for traction
  • Flex leather — for thin-soled premium brands
  • Heels — rubber, or combination with a rubber plug
  • Sole savers — thin rubber half-sole to protect a new outsole
  • Custom-mixed dye to match a dress or swatch — fabric or leather
  • Four-step hand shine: clean, strip old wax, condition, seal and brush
Allen Edmonds · Ferragamo · Santoni · Cole Haan · Bally · Johnston & Murphy · Bruno Magli · Mezlan

How it works

No surprises at the counter.

We explain the job and the price options before anything is touched — what gets repaired, how, why and how much.

  1. Bring it in, or ship it

    Walk in during shop hours, or call for packing instructions and mail it from anywhere in the US or Canada.

  2. Free estimate

    We look it over, walk you through the options, and give you a price. No charge for the look.

  3. The repair

    Done at our bench with the best materials available. Most jobs are back within a week.

  4. Guaranteed

    Every job is guaranteed for workmanship and materials. If it isn't right, bring it back.

Our story

Chicago to Phoenix.

Cobbler's Den was founded by Mack, a cobbler from Chicago who moved to the Scottsdale–Phoenix area with his wife and children and built a reputation for quality and service.

He ran a very successful shop on Camelback near the Biltmore for more than fifteen years, sold it in 2003, and opened Cobbler's Den on Tatum just north of Cactus — where we've been ever since.

His son Chris now runs the day-to-day and holds to the standards his father set.

"You'll know exactly what will be repaired, how, why, and how much."— Chris, Owner
