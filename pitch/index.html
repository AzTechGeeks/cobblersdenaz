The brief

Same business, same content, a site that works on the device your customers actually use.

Nothing was invented. Every service, brand, award and line of your story came from cobblersdenaz.com — cleaned up, de-duplicated, and put where a phone user can reach it.

What changed

Four things the old site couldn't do.

01

Works on a phone

Someone with a broken strap is searching on their phone, not a desktop. Layout, type size, tap targets and the bottom action bar are all designed at 390px first and scaled up — not the other way round.

02

Leads with the work

Hero that displays a cobbler working that leads into the redesign, six photo tiles for what you repair, your commercial's counter shot in the story. The old site had four thumbnails smaller than an app icon.

03

Says one true thing

The hours conflict, the closed landmark, the closed second location and the "three generations" line are gone. Hours: Mon–Fri 8:30–5, Sat 9–3. Location: across from Paradise Valley Mall. Two generations at the bench. "We explain the job and the price options before anything is touched."

04

Costs nothing to run

One HTML file with the photos, badges and logos embedded. No WordPress to update, no plugin to break, no hosting tier to upgrade. Drop it on any web host and it's live.
